**About Nahj al-Balagha**
Compiled in the 10th century by Sharif al-Radi, Nahj al-Balagha preserves the speeches, letters, and sayings of Imam Ali ibn Abi Talib (600-661 CE), cousin and son-in-law of Prophet Muhammad. Renowned for its eloquence and depth, it addresses universal themes: justice, governance, piety, knowledge, and the human condition.

Whether you're a scholar, student, or seeker of wisdom, Nahj al-Balagha offers profound insights that remain relevant across centuries.
